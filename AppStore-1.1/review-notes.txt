OrbitDeck is an amateur-radio satellite tracking and operating app. No account or login is needed. On a new install a station readiness checklist opens once; tap Done. The menu button (top left on iPhone; the sidebar on iPad) reaches every screen.

WITHOUT RADIO HARDWARE
Tracking, pass prediction, maps, planning tools, calculators and the log need no hardware.
The audio cards (Pass recording, SSTV, FT4, CW, Packet / APRS) are hidden by default unless a USB or network audio interface is connected. To see them, open Settings > Audio features and set Pass recording, SSTV decode, FT4, Packet / APRS and CW decode to "Always". They then appear on Home using the built-in microphone.
Live Activity (iOS 18 or later): Settings > Display > "Next pass on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island" is on by default. Lock the device to see the next-pass countdown.

BACKGROUND AUDIO (UIBackgroundModes: audio)
A satellite pass lasts 8-15 minutes and the operator routinely locks the screen partway through. The app keeps recording and processing audio for the whole pass: (1) Pass recording saves the radio's received audio to an AAC file; (2) the SSTV, FT4, CW and packet decoders process received audio continuously, and FT4 and packet also play transmit audio out to the radio; (3) Remote voice streams a network radio's received audio to the speaker. Interrupting any of these loses the pass.
To verify with the built-in microphone:
1. Settings > Audio features > Pass recording > "Always".
2. Go to Home; the Pass recording card appears.
3. Tap Record and allow microphone access. The timer starts.
4. Go to the Home Screen or lock the device. The orange microphone indicator stays on.
5. Wait 30 seconds, reopen OrbitDeck: the timer has kept counting. Tap Stop, then open Log > Pass recordings & voice memos and play the file, including the audio captured in the background.
SSTV decode set to "Always" behaves the same way. Rig (CAT) and rotator control are foreground-only and do not rely on the audio mode.
BACKGROUND FETCH (UIBackgroundModes: fetch) is used only for BGAppRefreshTask to keep the next-pass Live Activity current from data already on the device.

PERMISSIONS
- Local network: only when the user connects a LAN device: an Icom network radio or rigctld server (CAT), a network rotator, a KISS TNC over TCP, or their own ADS-B receiver. The optional rigctld/rotctld servers (TCP 4532/4533) are off by default; each new client must be approved (allow once, always, deny; denied after 30 s or in the background); VPN and cellular clients are refused.
- Bluetooth: only to connect a Bluetooth LE serial adapter for CAT or a rotator, the IC-705/IC-905's built-in Bluetooth, or a Bluetooth LE KISS TNC.
- Microphone: iOS treats a USB audio interface as a microphone. Used only while recording, decoding, making a memo or talking; decoding is on-device and audio is never uploaded.
- Location (when in use): the observer position for pass predictions; a grid square or coordinates can be entered instead.
- Notifications (local only): pass alarms, activation reminders and packet message alerts.
- Calendar (write-only) and Photos (add-only): only when the user adds a pass to the calendar or exports an SSTV image.

TRANSMIT FEATURES
FT4, packet/APRS, remote voice, Tune and network PTT key a radio and require an amateur radio license and the user's own radio. All are off by default: FT4 Transmit is an explicit toggle, every transmit path needs the user's callsign and a connected radio, and keying from network clients is a separate opt-in with a 3-minute limit. One transmitter authority enforces maximum key times and unkeys on Stop, disconnect and link loss. They cannot be exercised without a radio.

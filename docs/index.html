A complete amateur-radio satellite station for iPhone and iPad — passes and Doppler, rig and rotator control, FT4, CW, packet/APRS and SSTV, logging with LoTW and awards, and a Live Activity that counts down to your next pass.

About OrbitDeck

OrbitDeck computes passes, Doppler, footprints and link budgets from live orbital elements and runs natively on iPhone and iPad. Version 1.1 turns it from a tracker into a station controller: it decodes CW and packet on the downlink, drives two radios and a rotator, switches satellites automatically as passes begin, keeps your log ready for LoTW and awards, and shows the next pass on the Lock Screen while the app is closed. It is a companion to the OrbitDeck desktop app and CardSat by Paul Stoetzer, N8HM.

What's new in 1.1

The biggest update since 1.0. The highlights are below; the documentation covers every screen and setting.

CW decoder

Copy Morse on the downlink. The DeepCW neural decoder is the default and handles weak signals and uneven fists; the Classic decoder is still available. Tap a decoded callsign to log it.

Packet & APRS

Built-in 1200 and 9600 baud modems or a KISS TNC over Bluetooth LE or TCP, APRS messaging and beacons through satellites and the ISS, a map of heard stations, a per-pass log and an optional receive-only satgate.

Awards & a better log

VUCC grids, WAS, DXCC and per-satellite totals; a LoTW station-location picker with IOTA; voice memos that become QSO entries; and PDF log reports.

Station control

Two-radio stations with satellite rigs as legs, a live tune-mode selector, a one-tap Stop all, and rigctld/rotctld servers (with client approval) so desktop programs can share OrbitDeck's radio and rotator links.

Automatic satellite switching

SatPC32-style priority switching, with a KlaTrack-style pass timeline on Home. The radio, rotator and Live Activity follow along.

Rotator upgrades

Manual control, position read-back with a stall warning, a self-test, and Sun and Moon targets for sun-noise checks and EME.

Live Activity, Dynamic Island & Apple Watch

The next pass on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, now running through the pass and on to the next while OrbitDeck is closed, with a compact layout for the Apple Watch Smart Stack.

Planning extras

Weather and cloud cover for visible passes, activation reminders, a Nearby & DX hub, backup & restore, in-app Help and a station readiness checklist.

Safer transmitting

Only one feature can key the transmitter at a time, Stop and disconnect always send PTT-off first, every key has a time limit, and a network client needs your approval before it can use your radio or rotator.

Everything the satellite operator needs

Every feature area in OrbitDeck 1.1, at a glance. Follow the links for the detailed guides.

Track & plan

Track & predict

Live look angles, range, range rate and footprint; next passes with quality scoring and elevation profiles; a daily schedule, multi-day pass progression, ground track, all-sky radar, 3D globe and polar sky plot. Geostationary satellites such as QO-100 are shown as continuously visible.

Automatic satellite switching

Up to 9 priority satellites, switched SatPC32-style as each reaches your switch-in elevation, with "take over at", finish-current-pass and pause-after-manual-pick options. A 12-hour simulated plan shows what will happen. How it works

Pass timeline

A KlaTrack-style elevation timeline of your favorites' passes on Home, over 3, 6 or 12 hours, in per-satellite colors or sunlit/eclipse shading. Tap a pass to select that satellite.

Live Activity, Dynamic Island & Apple Watch

A countdown to the next AOS, then time left and a progress bar through the pass, with AOS/LOS times, directions and the pass after it. It keeps going while OrbitDeck is closed, dims with "Open OrbitDeck to update" when out of date, and opens that satellite when tapped. Details

Weather & cloud cover

Local weather from Open-Meteo on the Planning screen, and a forecast cloud-cover badge on optically visible passes and Sun/Moon transits for the next 48 hours.

Activations & skeds

Upcoming hams.at activations with "Can I work it?", your own activations and skeds stored on the device, reminders 60, 30 and 10 minutes before, and a cached feed when you're offline. Post your own alerts with your hams.at key.

AMSAT status

The status.amsat.org board over a 3–72 hour window, with "heard" ages, reporter grid counts and heard marks in the Satellites list. File a public report from Home or the AMSAT Status screen, always after confirming.

Nearby & DX

One hub for receive-only APRS-IS stations around you, a DX cluster, aircraft from your own ADS-B receiver on the local network, and a telnet console.

Radio & station control

Doppler & calibration

Live Doppler for every transponder with an SSB | CW switch, passband tuning slider and a no-CAT HOLD/TUNE calculator. Per-satellite calibration is shared across every Doppler screen, with a bulk Calibrations editor and CSV import/export.

Rig control (CAT)

Doppler-tune Icom, Yaesu and Kenwood radios, or any Hamlib radio via rigctld, over a BLE serial adapter, Icom network (Wi-Fi), or the IC-705/IC-905's built-in Bluetooth. Split-VFO Doppler on one radio, per-satellite CTCSS and data modes for FT4 and packet. Rig Control guide

Two radios & satellite-rig legs

Use a satellite rig as one leg of a two-radio station, for example an IC-821 on the 2 m uplink and an IC-705 on the 10 m downlink for AO-7 Mode A. Setup mistakes such as one adapter for both radios are caught before connecting.

Tune modes & Beacon

Switch Doppler live from the Home rig card: Full (follow downlink), Full (follow uplink), Downlink only, Uplink only or Hold. A one-tap Beacon button tunes the satellite's beacon or telemetry downlink.

Stop all

One button on Home releases PTT, stops FT4, packet and remote-voice transmit, ends rigctld/rotctld client control, disconnects the radio, and parks and disconnects the rotator.

Network servers with approval

Built-in rigctld (TCP 4532) and rotctld (TCP 4533) servers let Gpredict, SatPC32, WSJT-X or a logger on your LAN share OrbitDeck's links. New clients wait for your Allow once, Always allow or Deny; network PTT is a separate opt-in with a 3-minute limit.

Rotator control

GS-232 (including azimuth-only), Easycomm, SPID, SAEBRTrack, rotctld, PstRotator and OZ9AAR URC. Manual jog and go-to, position read-back with a stall warning, a self-test, Sun/Moon/fixed-bearing targets, and automatic retry of failed points. Rotator guide

Digital modes & audio

CW decoder

DeepCW, a neural decoder by e04, copies 400–1200 Hz including weak signals and uneven fists, about two seconds behind the audio. The Classic decoder offers tone search with AFC, 50/100/200 Hz filters, squelch and an adjustable speed range (5–60 WPM) or fixed speed. CW guide

Packet & APRS

Built-in 1200 baud AFSK and 9600 baud G3RUH modems, or a KISS TNC over Bluetooth LE (including the B.B. Link for the TH-D74/D75) or TCP (Direwolf, UZ7HO soundmodem). APRS or UISS-style messages, macros, timed beacons and automatic QSO logging.

APRS map, pass log & alerts

A map of heard stations, objects and items; a log of every frame per pass with statistics and CSV export; alerts for messages to you and your digipeated frames; and an opt-in, receive-only satgate to APRS-IS.

FT4

Full-duplex FT4 on linear transponders with signal subtraction, a deep "Decode again" pass, DT filtering, Band Activity shading for new calls and grids, wait-and-pounce, a TX watchdog (6 minutes by default) and a Tune carrier of up to 10 seconds. FT4 guide

SSTV

Decodes satellite and ISS SSTV, now including Martin M3/M4 and Scottie S3/S4, with damaged-header fallback, noisy-line repair, combined repeat copies, and automatic start and stop by elevation.

Recording & audio health

Record a pass over USB or Icom network audio (or the built-in microphone). OrbitDeck detects a stalled input and restarts it, and keeps the screen awake while any decoder or recording runs. Digital Modes guide

Logging & awards

Logging & uploads

An on-device QSO log with LoTW upload signed on your device, Cloudlog/Wavelog upload, ADIF import/export, DXCC and country on every QSO and default RST by mode. An unreadable log is set aside, never overwritten, and a Cloudlog/Wavelog upload is marked done only when the server confirms it imported every QSO. Logging guide

Awards

Totals for QSOs, satellites, VUCC grids, WAS (50 states plus DC) and DXCC, worked versus LoTW-confirmed, with per-satellite drill-down and CSV/PDF export.

LoTW location & IOTA

Pick your DXCC entity and its subdivisions as LoTW lists them (US counties, Canadian provinces, Japanese prefectures and city/gun/ku, Russian oblasts and more), plus an optional IOTA reference.

Voice memos

Speak a note during a pass; it's saved with your recordings and a QSO stub with the time, satellite, mode and frequencies goes into the log for you to finish later.

PDF reports

Print or share a QSO log report, awards, a station readiness report, favorites' element sets, a Support AMSAT sheet and a contact card with a QR code.

Maps, tools & learning

Location tools

A location converter for Maidenhead, decimal degrees, DMS, DDM, Plus Code, UTM, MGRS and USNG, and grid distance & bearing with short and long path, QRZ lookup and a "Point rotator" button.

Workable states & DXCC

See which grids, US states and DXCC entities a pass reaches, tested against real boundaries, with CSV/PDF export. The globe shows a DX footprint of mutual visibility, and the Sky Map adds a star field.

Plan & analyze

Orbital analysis with element-set number, epoch age and launch siblings; J2 rates, beta angle and eclipse fraction; equator crossings, Sun/Moon transits, conjunctions, a debris-group screen and comet positions.

Propagation & space weather

Solar flux, Kp and A from NOAA SWPC and sunspot number from hamqsl.com (N0NBH), now with trend changes, plus an MUF/HF path tool, 6 m/HF outlook and aurora likelihood.

Classic OSCARLOCATOR

An on-screen simulator in polar and QTH-centered projections, printable overlays, and the Reference Orbits schedule.

Bench tools

60+ calculators, a receipt-tape scientific calculator, a graphing calculator with zeros, intersections, slope, integral and CSV export, Tiny BASIC and a DXCC lookup.

Learn & References

Animated orbit types, an AMSAT Fox CubeSat anatomy, an introduction to the AMSAT CubeSat Simulator, interactive lessons, and reference tables including a worldwide band plan.

Notes

Plain-text notes for skeds, frequencies, grids to work or a pass plan, also available in the Files app under On My iPhone → OrbitDeck → Notes.

Setup, help & data

Help & readiness checklist

Searchable in-app Help, and a station readiness checklist (shown on first run for new installs) that marks what's ready and what needs attention, with a tap to fix each item and a PDF report.

Backup & restore

Save your station, favorites, calibrations, CAT, rotator, auto-switch, digital-mode and logging setup to one JSON file, optionally with the QSO log, and restore it after a preview. Passwords and keys are never included. A factory reset is there too.

Units & times

Metric, US customary or automatic by region, throughout the app and calculators; exports stay metric. Times in UTC or your local zone.

Station & grid squares

A fixed site or your current location, entered by coordinates or grid, with saved secondary sites. VUCC grid-line and corner recognition, a Grid Finder, and current DXCC, state and county in LoTW's names.

Catalog, offline & export

Elements from AMSAT, CelesTrak or your own URL, within CelesTrak's usage policy (at most one download per dataset every two hours). Imported TLE/OMM elements are kept until the feed catches up, malformed entries are skipped and counted, and cached data keeps core features working offline. PDF and CSV/XLSX exports.

Safety

One transmitter at a time ("Transmitter in use by …"), Stop and disconnect always unkey (an unconfirmed unkey is retried and flagged), no key longer than 4 minutes, voice transmit limited to 3 minutes, and FT4 Tune only with Transmit on and a callsign set. Packet monitoring works without a callsign; transmitting needs one.

Requirements

OrbitDeck runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. An internet connection is used to download orbital elements, space weather and the online services you choose to use; once cached, core tracking works offline.

  • The next-pass Live Activity and Dynamic Island need iOS 18 or later; on iOS 17 the rest of the app works without them. The Dynamic Island appears only on iPhone models that have one.
  • Starting the next pass's Live Activity automatically while OrbitDeck is closed needs iOS 26 or later; iOS starts it 15 minutes before AOS.
  • The Apple Watch Smart Stack layout appears on a paired Apple Watch whose software shows iPhone Live Activities (watchOS 11 or later).
  • Rig and rotator control need a supported Bluetooth LE serial adapter, an Icom network or Bluetooth radio, or a Hamlib server on your network, and run while OrbitDeck is in the foreground.
  • The audio features work best with a USB audio interface or an Icom network-audio radio. Set a feature to Always in Settings → Audio features to use the built-in microphone instead; remote voice needs an Icom network radio.
